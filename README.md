## 2. Four Steps to Build Your eBook Income System

### Step 1: Strategic Niche Selection — Focus on Pain Points, Not Just Passion

Many authors fail because they write about what *they* want to write, rather than what *others* are willing to pay for. High-converting topics generally fall into three categories:

* **Wealth & High-Value Skills:** e.g., *Practical Prompt Engineering for Beginners*, *Python Automation for Office Efficiency*.
* **Productivity & Problem-Solving:** e.g., *The 30-Day Anti-Procrastination Guide*, *Executive Time Management Frameworks*.
* **Niche Lifestyle & Hobbies:** e.g., *Indoor Hydroponic Gardening at Home*, *Low-Carb Meal Prep & Nutrition Guides*.

> **The Topic Formula:** **Target Audience + Clear Pain Point + Measurable Outcome**
> *Example:* "A 7-Day Personal Finance Roadmap (Outcome) for Complete Beginners (Audience) to Overcome Money Anxiety (Pain Point)."
